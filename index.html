📱 Getting Started

Before using the application, ensure your Android device is properly prepared:

  1. Enable Developer Options by tapping Build Number 7 times in Settings.
  2. Navigate to Developer Options and toggle it ON.
  3. Enable USB Debugging.
  4. Connect your phone to the PC via USB cable.
  5. When prompted on your phone, tap Allow USB Debugging (check "Always allow" for future sessions).
💡 Pro Tip: If connection fails, try disabling USB Debugging Security or enabling Disable Permission Monitoring in Developer Options.

🖥️ Interface Overview

The main window is divided into logical panels for quick navigation:

  • Top Bar: Displays your unique Hardware ID, connection status (ADB/COM), refresh controls, and theme toggle.
  • Left Panel: Package list with checkboxes, search bar, and color-coded legend. Right-click any app for quick actions.
  • Right Panel: Device information, progress tracking, and real-time operation logs.
  • Bottom Bar: Scrolling announcements & tips. Click to open the full Tips & Tricks dialog.

🎨 Color Legend & Exclusions

Apps are automatically color-coded based on your protection settings:

Removable (Safe to act on)
Clean Excluded
Uninstall Excluded
Both Excluded

Clicking a color in the legend filters the list to show only matching apps. Click the same color again to reset.
